About Sculptor AI

            
Sculptor AI (SculptorAI) is a nonprofit AI research lab based in Seattle, Washington. We build machine learning models, tools, and infrastructure, and publish the work in the open.

            

                
research

                
    
                    
  • SunfishA coding-first diffusion language model for consumer hardware
    • 
                    
  • Brown dwarf atmospheresCalibrated uncertainty for atmospheric retrievals from spectra, on arXiv
    • 
                    
  • Hyperspectral spoilage forecastingPredicting how many days each individual apple has left
    • 
                    
  • Weather forecastingFine-tuned time-series models that correct weather guidance, live at Indianapolis as Indystral
    • 
                    
  • Autonomous drivingTurnPilot, our fork of openpilot, and lightd, its traffic light detector
    •
