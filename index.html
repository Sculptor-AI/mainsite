About

                
Sculptor is a student-run AI research group. We build models, tools, and infrastructure, and
                    release everything open source.

            


            

                
Research

                
What we're working on now.

            


            

                
Sunfish

                
A diffusion model for high-fidelity text synthesis, focused on architectural efficiency and
                    style adaptability.

            

            

                
Brown Dwarf Classifier

                
Predicting the physical properties of brown dwarfs from 100 million synthetic spectra.

            


            

                
Autonomous Driving

                
Ongoing research in perception and planning for autonomous vehicles, from simulation to
                    small-scale test platforms.
