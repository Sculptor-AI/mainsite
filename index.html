Research

                
What we're working on now.

            


            

                
Sunfish

                
A diffusion model for high-fidelity text synthesis, focused on architectural efficiency and
                    style adaptability.

                
                    
                    Sculptor-AI/Sunfish
                
            

            

                
Brown Dwarf Atmospheres

                
Predicting brown dwarf atmospheres from their spectra. Randomizing molecular opacities during
                    training teaches the network to account for line-list disagreement, lifting coverage of nominal
                    90% carbon-to-oxygen intervals from 43% to 82%.

                
                    
                    arXiv:2609.01665
                
            


            

                
Autonomous Driving

                
Research in perception and planning for autonomous vehicles, from simulation to small-scale
                    test platforms. TurnPilot, our fork of openpilot, is coming soon.
