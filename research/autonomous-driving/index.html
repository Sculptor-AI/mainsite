Autonomous driving

            
Sculptor AI researches perception and planning for autonomous vehicles, from simulation to small-scale test platforms. Our main project is TurnPilot, a fork of openpilot with navigation and traffic light handling. TurnPilot is coming soon.

lightd-v4

                
    
                    
  • What it isA small traffic light detector for comma cameras that finds lights in a forward camera image and reads their color and arrow shape
    • 
                    
  • ScopeValidated for clear daytime driving only. Night, precipitation, and other cameras are not validated.
    • 
                    
  • ArchitectureEfficientNet-B0 CenterNet, distilled from a ConvNeXt-T teacher
    • 
                    
  • Size3.7M parameters, 8.7 GFLOPs, ONNX
    • 
                    
  • AP0.898 on a hand-labeled comma10k test set (240 frames, 445 lights), 0.856 on LISA, Bosch, and S2TLD test splits
    • 
                    
  • Color100% correct on detected lights in that comma test, with no red read as green
