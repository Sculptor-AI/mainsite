Brown dwarf atmospheres

            
Molecular line lists disagree enough to shift the carbon-to-oxygen ratio (C/O) retrieved from a brown dwarf's spectrum. A retrieval that assumes a single list leaves that error out of its error bars. This paper moves the fix into training, so a neural network returns intervals that already account for it.

method

                

                    
Classical marginalization reruns a full retrieval under many opacity realizations for every object.
                        Here that cost moves into simulation. While generating the training set, opacities are randomized with
                        smooth multiplicative Fourier fields in log frequency plus a discrete mixture of line lists, under a
                        deliberately broad prior informed by measured differences between lists. A neural posterior estimator
                        trained on that set learns a posterior that is already marginalized.

                

            

            

                
results

                

                    
Coverage of nominal 90% C/O intervals, from the paper:

                    
TestMarginalizedControl
Single network, perturbed held-out spectra (n=2048)82.4%43.3%
Three-seed ensemble, perturbed spectra89.4%61.4%

                    
On clean data the control ensemble reaches 93.2%, which isolates the deficit as opacity-induced.
                        In simulation, real JWST bad-pixel masks with median filling drop the marginalized ensemble from 89.4% to
                        68.3%. An ensemble retrained with mask augmentation holds 91.8% under the full deployment path (n=1024).

                    
On one pipeline-combined JWST/NIRSpec G395H spectrum each of 14 late-T and Y dwarfs, the mask-augmented
                        ensemble returns C/O medians from 0.24 to 0.62. It contains the Hood et al. (2024) value of 0.53 for the
                        T8 benchmark 2MASS J0415-0935, which every fixed-opacity control network excludes. Its 90% intervals span
                        about 0.43 of the prior width, so the paper treats this as a consistency check rather than a validation.
