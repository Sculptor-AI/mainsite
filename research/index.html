Research

            
What Sculptor AI (SculptorAI), a nonprofit AI research lab in Seattle, is working on now.

            

                
projects

                
    
                    
  • SunfishAn experimental, coding-first diffusion language model aimed at very low single-user latency on consumer hardware.
    • 
                    
  • Brown dwarf atmospheresPredicting brown dwarf atmospheres from their spectra. Randomizing molecular opacities during training teaches the network to account for line-list disagreement, lifting coverage of nominal 90% carbon-to-oxygen intervals from 43% to 82%.
    • 
                    
  • Hyperspectral spoilage forecastingFruit is managed by the batch, but apples from the same crate can spoil days apart. We're using hyperspectral imaging to forecast how many days each individual apple has left, and will release the dataset and code.
    • 
                    
  • Weather forecastingTeaching time-series models where the weather models go wrong. Indra is a family of tiny, general weather models with open weights, at 3M and 1.5M parameters. Indra-3M runs our live Indianapolis forecast.
    • 
                    
  • Autonomous drivingTurnPilot, our fork of openpilot, and lightd, a small traffic light detector for comma cameras.
    •
