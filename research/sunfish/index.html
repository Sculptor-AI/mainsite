Sunfish

            
Sunfish is an experimental, coding-first diffusion language model from Sculptor AI, aimed at very low single-user latency on consumer hardware.

            

                
status

                
    
                    
  • StageIn development. No weights have been released yet.
    • 
                    
  • Primary targetRTX 5080 with 16 GB of memory
    • 
                    
  • Secondary target24 GB Apple Silicon laptop
    • 
                    
  • Codegithub.com/Sculptor-AI/Sunfish
    • 
                

            

            

                
design

                
    
                    
  • BaseA text-only, structurally pruned derivative of Google's DiffusionGemma architecture
    • 
                    
  • Experts per layer128 reduced to 32, keeping the always-active shared expert
    • 
                    
  • Routing4 experts per token initially, down from 8
    • 
                    
  • Vision towerRemoved for the coding and agentic target
    • 
                    
  • Parameters8,114,384,892 total, 3,118,575,612 active (audited against the released shard headers)
    • 
                

            

            

                
why-pruning

                

                    
Starting from a small autoregressive model would mean teaching it the diffusion objective,
                        bidirectional denoising, self-conditioning, and prefill behavior from nothing. Pruning lets Sunfish inherit
                        those behaviors from a model that is already trained, and makes expert selection and recovery training the
                        main research problem.

                

            

            

                
first-gates

                

                    
The project does not spend on a large training run until the checkpoint transformation is lossless on a
                        no-prune control and zero-shot pruning data supports a specific candidate. The first gates are to reproduce
                        the upstream 4-bit model on the target hardware, instrument router use across coding, agentic, and general
                        prompts, and run a controlled pruning ablation measuring quality, memory, and latency at each step.
