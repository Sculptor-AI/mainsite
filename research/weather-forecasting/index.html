Weather forecasting

            
We fine-tune time-series models to learn where the professional weather models go wrong. Indra is a family of tiny, general weather models with open weights: Indra-3M and Indra-1.5M. Indra-3M runs our live forecast, which covers 2,407 airports in the lower 48 states. Your browser downloads the model and computes the forecast itself. We have scored or trained the model at 49 of those airports.

            

                
what has been measured

                

                    
Airports are what Indra was trained and tested on, because they have the long, reliable records and guidance archives that training and scoring need. They are not its purpose. Every score on this page comes from an airport, and nothing else has been measured. Indra depends on US forecast guidance, and the script only reads airport data so far. A station away from an airport would have to use a nearby airport's guidance, and we haven't measured how it does there.

Indra-3M

                
    
                    
  • What it isA 12 MB model that forecasts temperature, dew point, wind, and pressure for each of the next 72 hours at a station, with an 80% range around each forecast
    • 
                    
  • Size2,999,627 parameters
    • 
                    
  • BasePruned and fine-tuned from Timestral-5M by Mistral AI
    • 
                    
  • InputsA station's last 90 days of observations, plus US forecast guidance: the National Blend of Models, GFS MOS, and NAM MOS. The script reads airport data so far.
    • 
                    
  • LicenseModified MIT, the same as Timestral, with Mistral AI's attribution notice and a condition on companies with more than $20 million in monthly revenue
    • 
                    
  • Not forSafety decisions, including aviation and emergencies. It's a research model.
    • 
                    
  • Weights and cardhuggingface.co/Sculptor-AI/Indra-3M
    • 
                

            

            

                
Indra-1.5M

                
    
                    
  • What it isA 6 MB model that forecasts temperature, dew point, wind, and pressure for each of the next 72 hours at a station, with an 80% range around each forecast
    • 
                    
  • Size1,500,874 parameters
    • 
                    
  • Next to Indra-3MHalf the size, with 1.2% more temperature error on the final test
    • 
                    
  • BasePruned and fine-tuned from Timestral-5M by Mistral AI
    • 
                    
  • InputsA station's last 90 days of observations, plus US forecast guidance: the National Blend of Models, GFS MOS, and NAM MOS. The script reads airport data so far.
    • 
                    
  • LicenseModified MIT, the same as Timestral, with Mistral AI's attribution notice and a condition on companies with more than $20 million in monthly revenue
    • 
                    
  • Not forSafety decisions, including aviation and emergencies. It's a research model.

Indra-3M record

                

                    
Average temperature error, 1 to 72 hours ahead, over November 2025 to October 2026, at six airports it never trained on. Scored once on the locked model. Lower is better.

                    
AirportIndra-3MNational BlendImprovement
Columbus (CMH)2.04°F2.27°F10.2%
Chicago O'Hare (ORD)2.08°F2.31°F10.2%
Denver (DEN)2.79°F3.29°F15.4%
Miami (MIA)1.42°F1.59°F10.3%
Seattle (SEA)1.61°F1.79°F9.7%
Phoenix (PHX)1.70°F2.03°F15.9%

                    
These six weren't completely untouched: we had used them to check earlier models, and a 5.2M version from this same work had already been scored on this period. So before scoring, we also wrote down 12 other airports that no model in this project had trained on, been tuned on, or been checked against, and scored them once over the same year, with 240 forecasts each. Across all 12, its temperature error was 11.7% lower than the National Blend overall, and
                        3.7% to 15.4% lower by airport.
