Weather forecasting

            
We fine-tune time-series models to learn where the professional weather models go wrong. Indystral is our hourly forecast for Indianapolis International Airport (KIND), and Indra-3M is a separate, much smaller model with open weights for US airports.

            

                
Indra-3M

                
    
                    
  • What it isA 12 MB model that forecasts temperature, dew point, wind, and pressure for each of the next 72 hours at US airports, with an 80% range around each forecast
    • 
                    
  • Size2,999,627 parameters
    • 
                    
  • BasePruned and fine-tuned from Timestral-5M by Mistral AI
    • 
                    
  • InputsAn airport's last 90 days of observations, plus US forecast guidance: the National Blend of Models, GFS MOS, and NAM MOS
    • 
                    
  • LicenseModified MIT, the same as Timestral, with Mistral AI's attribution notice and a condition on companies with more than $20 million in monthly revenue
    • 
                    
  • Not forSafety decisions, including aviation and emergencies. It's a research model.
    • 
                    
  • Weights and cardhuggingface.co/Sculptor-AI/Indra-3M
    •

Indystral inputs

                

                    
The models read recent measurements at the airport alongside four forecast sources over those same days and
                        the next 72 hours: the HRRR, the National Blend of Models, and the GFS and NAM model output statistics. Seeing
                        each source's recent forecasts next to what happened lets them learn where it tends to be wrong.

                

            

            

                
Indystral training

                
    
                    
  • TrainHourly data from 2008 to October 2024, where every starting point saw only data published by then
    • 
                    
  • ChooseNovember 2024 to October 2025, used to choose between versions
    • 
                    
  • TestNovember 2025 to October 2026, left untouched until the models were final
